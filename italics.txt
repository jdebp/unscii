Italics (ornate forms, not merely oblique forms) for a small set of letters.

Not based upon any specific home computer font, but italicized according to some general principles.

Uprights become 2:2:2 or 1:2:1:2 slopes.
Slopes become diagonals.
Square 2 by 2 pixel dots in punctuation scale up to more like circles.

Several glyphs are based around an 8 by 8 circle or a 6 by 10 oval.

Some stroke widths on uppercase are widened to help distinguish them from lowercase.

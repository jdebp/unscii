Square forms for a small set of letters.

Not based upon any specific home computer font, but sort of resembles some games.

Everything here fits into a 14 by 14 box.
Most lowercase also fits (sans ascenders/descenders) into a 12 by 10 box.

Where possible, a glyph hugs the side of the box and avoids diagonal lines.
Square corners are used unless they introduce ambiguity.

This is the ROM font from the Motorola MC 6670P.

It had 128 glyphs with lowercase including the ISO-2047 control character symbols (codepoints 0x00-0x1a and 0x7f).
The TRS-80 itself had no way to show the ISO-2047 glyphs.

It is (with a handful of exceptions) actually a 5 by 7 font with 3 columns and 1 row of guttering.

The MC 6670P was used in the TSR-80 model 1, and was a custom version of the MC 6670.
The TRS-80 model 1 used the 1963 version of ASCII, like the Commodore PET.
We have added in the original MC 6670/MC 6674 glyphs in their ISO 8859-1 positions.

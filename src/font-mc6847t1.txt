This is the font per the Motorola MC 6847T1 data sheet.

The MC 6847T1 was an update to the original MC 6847 as used in the Dragon 32/64 (and Tandy Color Computer).
It had 96 glyphs with lowercase instead of 64 glyphs with reverse video uppercase.
Several of the original's glyphs were modified.

It is actually a 5 by 8 font with 3 columns of guttering.

Notice that this is the 1963 version of ASCII, like the Commodore PET.
